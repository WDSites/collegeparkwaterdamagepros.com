Victorian Houses and Sears Bungalows: What Age Means for Water Damage

A house that is a century old does not dry the way a new one does. Original plaster walls, solid hardwood floors and older supply lines hold moisture and fail in different ways from drywall, laminate and plastic pipe. When a pipe fails in a bungalow, the question is usually how far the water traveled inside the wall and under the floor, not how much is on the surface.

That is why crews in older houses start with moisture mapping and take readings at several heights. Plaster that is still firm can often be dried in place, while crumbling plaster has to come out. Our water damage repair page explains how ceiling, wall and floor repair is sequenced after drying.

When Water Is Spreading in Hyattsville

If water is moving through a floor, ceiling or wall right now, treat it as an emergency and call the line. These steps help while you wait.

If Water Is Still Spreading

Close the valve

Find the main shutoff where the water line enters the house and turn it off. Switch off the leaking appliance too.

Check the power

Turn power off to wet areas only when the panel is dry and reachable. If water is near outlets or the panel, stay clear and call the utility or 911.

Lift what you can

Get books, shoes, papers and small furniture up off wet floors. Leave heavy furniture alone.

Record it

Photos and short videos of the water and the damage go first. Keep a list of what was wet.
